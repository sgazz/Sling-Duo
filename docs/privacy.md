# Sling Duo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Sling Duo is provided by Stanko Gazibaric. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad game.

## Information in the game

Sling Duo does not collect or transmit personal data from the game to us. It has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, user accounts, or online leaderboards.

Progress, unlocked caves, collected fragments, scores, Escape times, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this information. Your device backups may include this local data according to your Apple device and backup settings.

## Motion controls

When you choose Tilt controls, device motion is used to steer the climbers. Motion readings are processed on the device and are not transmitted to us or stored as a motion history. Slide controls provide a touch-based alternative.

## Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information. Support email is separate from data collected by the game. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

## Children

The game does not require personal information from players of any age. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, please contact us.

## Apple and website hosting

Apple handles App Store downloads and any diagnostics you choose to share under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website visitor information, including IP addresses, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not add website analytics or advertising trackers to these pages.

## Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game's privacy practices change.
