# Sling Duo Support

Two climbers. One elastic rope.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, contact Stanko Gazibaric at [sgazza@outlook.com](mailto:sgazza@outlook.com).

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short explanation of the issue. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

## How do I play?

Drag and release to launch the first climber. Steer toward a platform; catching it launches your partner automatically. Reach 150 catches to escape a cave and unlock the next expedition. After an escape, you can continue in Endless.

## Can I play without tilting?

Yes. Choose Slide in the opening guide, or open Pause and change Controls to Slide. Touch and drag horizontally during flight, and release to brake.

## Where are the instructions?

Open Pause and select How to Play.

## Why did the game pause?

Backgrounding the game pauses play. Tap Continue to resume after a countdown.

## Can I turn off sound?

Use the speaker control or the sound setting in Pause.

## Where is my progress saved?

Progress, records, and preferences are stored on this device. There is no in-game account or cross-device sync. Deleting the app can remove local data; the game has no server copy to restore. Turbo runs have separate records.

## Do I need internet access?

No. Gameplay works offline and does not require an account.
